Updated investment insight

BMRI’s growth accelerated across loans, earnings, and efficiency—but balance-sheet intensity rose.

H1 net profit rose 25.0% year over year, credit expanded 19.9%, and net interest income grew 8.0%. Returns and efficiency strengthened—ROA reached 3.10%, ROE 24.28%, BOPO fell to 57.59%, and CIR to 35.60%—while gross NPL improved to below 1%. My read remains positive: execution, asset quality, and operating leverage are strong. However, loan growth materially outpaced equity growth of 6.1%, consistent with LDR rising to 92.73% and KPMM declining to 17.52%. NIM stabilization, funding growth, and capital-buffer replenishment are the next key checks.
